Epic M: the radio's own sensors (#1660)

Offband theme, Modern look, shown at 1.25×. Every Heltec V4 build already probes 14 I2C sensors at boot (temperature, humidity, pressure, power monitors, IR temperature, distance) and sends their readings to the phone; the GUI shows none of them. Readings here are examples. Owner decisions, 2026-10-19: the Bosch parts are tried at 0x77 as well as 0x76; Home's Airtime cell becomes the consolidated sensor reading when one is found (5a, consolidating what fits); Units °F / °C with °F the default (6a); a GPS section (fix, satellites, altitude; never position) heads the page. BSEC2 air quality for the BME688 is a follow-up (#1663).
