Epic I: the SD card (#1433)

Offband theme, Modern look, shown at 1.25×. These are the I6 mockups (#1702) for the owner's answer before any screen code. Figures are examples. Owner decisions, 2026-10-21: the card can be swapped while the radio runs. On the first boot with a card, history is copied from flash to the card, with a notice if it's slow; flash keeps the newest 1,000 (option B). A missing card falls back to flash, and the header shows a red disk icon; a card that's present and healthy shows it green. When the card returns, what was written to flash syncs to the card. Settings gets an SD card page with status, usage and Format card.

What flash holds once the card is in use: B (owner, 2026-10-21)

A · Flash is only the fallback. After the move, every message lives on the card. Flash holds only what arrives while the card is out. If the card is pulled, the chats show only messages from after the pull until it returns.

B · Flash keeps the newest messages as it does today (chosen). Flash goes on holding the newest 1,000 messages. The card holds the full history, up to 5,000. If the card is pulled, the chats still show recent history and nothing goes blank. When it returns, the card catches up. The first-boot "move" becomes a copy, and Format card leaves the flash copy alone. It costs no more flash writes than today, because today's store already writes every message to flash.
