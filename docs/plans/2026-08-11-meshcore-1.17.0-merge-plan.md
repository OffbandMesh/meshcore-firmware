## 2. Resolution strategy, by group

Ordered by risk. Each group is a separate commit on the integration branch so a bad resolution can be reverted in isolation.

### Group A — CI workflows we deleted, upstream modified (4 files) · **Trivial**

`.github/workflows/build-{companion,repeater,room-server}-firmwares.yml`, `.github/workflows/pr-build-check.yml`

All four are `modify/delete`: deleted on our side, modified upstream. **Resolution: keep deleted** (`git rm`). Offband runs its own `ci.yml` + the `ci-green` aggregate (#477); re-adopting upstream's per-role workflows would add a second, conflicting CI surface.

Also `.github/actions/setup-build-environment/action.yml` (1 hunk, 17 lines) — take upstream's, we have no local requirement there.

### Group B — variant + root `platformio.ini` (6 files) · **Low**

`minewsemi_me25ls01` (3 hunks/20), `thinknode_m2` (1/11), `rak3401` (1/6), `t1000-e` (1/5), `rak4631` (1/5), root `platformio.ini` (2/30).

**Resolution: union.** Upstream adds flags/envs; Offband adds its own envs and build flags. Neither side is removing the other's. Root `platformio.ini` additionally carries our `native` test-env source list — that stays.

⚠ **Do not lose:** the `-D SAFEBOOT_*` per-variant values (#602/#621 lineage) and the Offband-only envs. Post-resolution check: env count per variant must be ≥ pre-merge.

### Group C — FEM/LNA (6 files) · **Medium** — decided in #629

`heltec_v4/{LoRaFEMControl.h,HeltecV4Board.cpp}`, `heltec_t096/{LoRaFEMControl.h,T096Board.cpp,T096Board.h}`, `heltec_tracker_v2/{HeltecTrackerV2Board.cpp,LoRaFEMControl.h}`

**Resolution per #629: take upstream's interface, keep the Offband implementation.**
- `mesh::MainBoard` virtuals (`setLoRaFemLnaEnabled` / `canControlLoRaFemLna` / `isLoRaFemLnaEnabled`) — **take upstream's**; they are what the rest of 1.17.0 calls through.
- `LoRaFEMControl.cpp` — **keep ours**; the logic is identical and ours adds `FEM_DEBUG_PROBE`, the #327 GPIO46 strapping audit, and a correction to a comment still factually wrong upstream.
- Method naming — **align to upstream** (`isLnaEnabled` → `isLNAEnabled`, `isLnaCanControl` gains `const`), re-pointing all Offband call sites (#197).

⚠ `heltec_t096` and `heltec_tracker_v2` must be inspected individually — **do not assume they mirror `heltec_v4`**.

### Group D — prefs core (4 files) · **🔴 HIGH** — this is #627

`src/helpers/CommonCLI.h` (1 hunk/154), `src/helpers/CommonCLI.cpp` (9/236), `examples/companion_radio/NodePrefs.h` (1/214), `examples/companion_radio/DataStore.cpp` (1/50)

The governing risk of the whole update. **Resolution:**
1. Adopt upstream's `ConfigSerializer`-based `NodePrefs` (both classes).
2. **Keep Offband's `loadPrefsInt()` offsets** — the legacy reader must describe the bytes our nodes actually wrote.
3. Wire in the merged `offband::identifyLegacyPrefs()` (`src/helpers/prefs/PrefsLayout.h`, landed in #636) to pick the layout, and **fail closed** on `PrefsLayout::Unknown` — do not migrate, leave the legacy file, log at error level.
4. Add the Offband-only fields to `structure()`: `ui_led_enabled`, `ui_display_mode`, `caplog_*`, `syslog_*` (Path A); `radio_fem_rxgain`, `caplog_*`, `notify_scope`, `button_actions`, `ui_*` (Path B).
5. Map `fem_rxgain` → `radio_fem_rxgain` (upstream PR #3137's fix; check whether it merged first and inherit it if so).
6. Retain `/com_prefs` and `/new_prefs` after migration.
7. Add the mtime check: a legacy file newer than `/prefs.json` means a downgrade cycle — warn, never silently discard.

**This group does not merge until #627's remaining scope is written and tested.** It is the one group where "resolve the conflict" and "implement the feature" are the same work.

### Group E — `SerialBLEInterface` (2 files) · **Medium**

`.h` (3 hunks/32), `.cpp` (3/84)

**Resolution: take upstream's FreeRTOS `StaticQueue_t` RX-queue rewrite, keep our `maxFrameSize()` / `maxFramePayload()` override (#453).** The two are orthogonal — queueing vs framing.

⚠ **Mandatory post-merge assertion:** the override must still be present and exercised. Upstream has no frame cap of its own, so silently losing ours re-introduces BLE frame clipping with nothing to fall back on. Covered by #630.

### Group F — `ESP32Board` (2 files) · **Medium**

`.cpp` (2 hunks; hunk 2 is **ours=520 / theirs=41**), `.h` (2/56)

`[verified]` The large hunk is our SafeFlash block (OTA-over-STA, boot rollback, NVS `SafetyEventLog`) sitting adjacent to 41 lines of upstream additions. **Additive on both sides — resolution is a union**, not an either/or.

⚠ Post-merge: `startOTAUpdateOverSTA`, `ROLLBACK_THRESHOLD` and `SafetyEventLog` must all still be present (#630).

### Group G — `src/MeshCore.h` (1 file) · **Medium**

1 hunk / 20 lines. **Union:** take upstream's three FEM/LNA `MainBoard` virtuals (Group C) plus any other new base-class members; keep Offband's additions. `FIRMWARE_VERSION` moves `v1.16.0` → `v1.17.0` — that is a **user-visible string change requiring owner sign-off before it ships** (#632).

### Group H — role `main.cpp` / `UITask.cpp` (7 files) · **Medium, spread wide**

`companion_radio/main.cpp` (7 hunks/198), `companion_radio/ui-orig/UITask.cpp` (2/283), `companion_radio/ui-new/UITask.cpp` (1/56), `simple_repeater/main.cpp` (3/71), `simple_repeater/UITask.cpp` (1/36), `simple_room_server/main.cpp` (1/8), `simple_secure_chat/main.cpp` (1/7), `simple_sensor/main.cpp` (1/7)

**Resolution: union, role by role.** Offband adds `crashLogStandardInit()`, caplog, observer wiring and button/UI dispatch; upstream adds its own init and new display/input support. Resolve each file against what that role actually needs — these are where a careless union silently drops an Offband init call.

⚠ **`crashLogStandardInit()` must survive in every role's `main.cpp`.** It is the boot path that writes the NVS `cw_boot` marker, which the #627 detector uses as its ESP32 corroborating signal. Losing it degrades the migration's disambiguation without any compile error.

## 3. Sequencing

1. **Groups A, B** — mechanical, land first, one commit each. Get the tree compiling.
2. **Groups E, F, G, H** — the additive unions. Full matrix after each.
3. **Group C** — FEM, with the #629 naming sweep.
4. **Group D last** — it is gated on #627's remaining implementation and carries the hardware gate (#631).

Rationale: everything except D is recoverable by re-resolving a file. D changes data on deployed devices.

## 5. Rollback

The integration branch is disposable: `git worktree remove` + `git branch -D` returns to `firmware-base` with nothing lost. Nothing is pushed until the branch builds green. Per-group commits mean a single bad resolution reverts without unwinding the whole merge.

For a device that has already migrated under a bad build, recovery is #634 — **not yet written**, and it is a gate on shipping, not on merging.

## 6. Not covered here
