🔧 **Offband firmware v1.7.1** — a patch on top of 1.7.0. One thing is fixed, and it is the one people were hitting: **indicator control.**

💡 **If you set the status LED off and your device said it didn't support that — that's this.** One capability flag answered two different questions, display control and LED control, and it was decided at build time from whether a display driver was compiled in. Fifteen boards satisfy that test with a placeholder driver that reports no display at all. So those boards advertised both controls, your client offered both, and the firmware refused the write it had just claimed to support. Seen on a Heltec T096, a SenseCAP T1000-E and a QCC badge.

✅ **Now:** display control and LED control are reported **separately**, and only when the board actually has them — decided while the board is running, not from how it was built. A display change on a screenless board is refused instead of being saved and read back as if it worked.

🔴 **And the transmit LED can finally be switched off.** 63 boards have one; exactly two of them honored the setting. It now lives in the shared board code, so most of the fleet gains it at once — including 16 nRF52 boards with a heartbeat LED: **T1000-E, RAK4631, the ThinkNode boards, XIAO nRF52**. Hardware-verified on a T1000-E. A board that genuinely can't control its LED now reports the LED as on, because it is — "I can't switch this" isn't "it's off". The QCC badge is the one deliberate exception.

⏳ **Not done yet:** a few boards drive their LED from their own transmit code and bypass the shared switch. Tracked, not in this release.

🔁 **Upgrading from 1.7.0** keeps your settings, contacts and channels, and no default changed. Older radios still read correctly from a newer client — the old single flag is kept as the fallback. Stock MeshCore clients are unaffected. The image set is unchanged from 1.7.0.
